Estimates use your uploaded usage history scaled to a full year (or the exact period covered if less than a year), applying each plan's tariff structure — using exact hour-by-hour matching for time-of-use plans when your usage data has hourly resolution, or a typical load-shape estimate otherwise (flagged per row) — plus daily supply charges, controlled load charges (when CL usage is uploaded), and any guaranteed discounts. Green power surcharges, one-off sign-up credits, and pay-on-time conditional discounts are not included. Plans from wholesale/variable-pricing retailers (e.g. Amber) show their filed reference rate, not real wholesale pricing, so treat those figures as a rough guide only. Check the plan details for anything you're shortlisting.

Plan data sourced from the AER's Energy Made Easy CDR API. This tool runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves this page.
