Your current plan (optional, for comparison)

So the results table can show how much better or worse other plans are, relative to what you're paying now with Alinta. Prefilled from your last bill — edit as needed.

Controlled load energy is assumed to already be included in your main usage file and main usage rate above, so the CL rate above is used only to show a rate comparison column — it isn't added into the cost total (that would double-count the energy). If your usage file includes a solar export column, entering your current feed-in rate here nets that credit out of your current plan's cost too, for a fair comparison. Leave the supply/usage fields blank to skip the comparison — the ranking of market plans will still work fine.

Alinta's own portal export combines controlled load into the same usage totals as your main meter — it isn't broken out separately. That means the CL rate above is only ever a daily charge comparison (c/day), never a validated energy-rate (c/kWh) comparison, since there's no way to isolate how many of those kWh were actually CL.

Estimates use your uploaded usage history scaled to a full year (or the exact period covered if less than a year), applying each plan's tariff structure — using exact hour-by-hour matching for time-of-use plans when your usage data has hourly resolution, or a typical load-shape estimate otherwise (flagged per row) — plus daily supply charges, controlled load charges (when CL usage is uploaded), and any guaranteed discounts, plus conditional pay-on-time/direct-debit discounts if you've ticked that box above. Green power surcharges are not included. One-off sign-up credits are excluded from "Est. annual cost" (they don't repeat every year, so including them would make a plan look cheapest for the wrong reason) but are reflected in "Year 1 cost $", a separate one-time-only figure. Plans from wholesale/variable-pricing retailers (e.g. Amber) show their filed reference rate, not real wholesale pricing, so treat those figures as a rough guide only. Usage cost diff and supply charge diff only compare the plan's peak/anytime rate and main daily charge against your current plan — "Other diff" is the remainder (off-peak/shoulder usage, controlled load charges, discounts) so the three always add up to the total yearly diff. Solar credit is estimated from your uploaded export data against each plan's feed-in tariff and is already netted into the annual cost above (shown as $0 if you have no solar data or a plan has no feed-in tariff, rather than hidden). Columns with no data for any plan (e.g. off-peak diff, if you haven't entered a current off-peak rate) are hidden automatically. Check the plan details for anything you're shortlisting.

Plan data sourced from the AER's Energy Made Easy CDR API. This tool runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves this page.
